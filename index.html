Turf Health

Mowing Height Rules for Australian Summers

Never remove more than one-third of the leaf blade at once. Keeping Buffalo turf slightly taller during December to February shades the soil and reduces evaporation.

Seasonal guide arrow_forward
Soil Biology

Why Aeration is Vital After Heavy Spring Rains

Compacted topsoil chokes root systems. Core aeration perforates the lawn surface allowing essential oxygen, water, and microbial nutrition down to root depth.

Seasonal guide arrow_forward
Design Layout

Creating Microclimates with Native Shrubbery

Strategically positioning Callistemon and Grevillea acts as windbreaks, preserving humidity for tender understory ferns and lowering alfresco summer heat.

Common Questions

Frequently asked inquiries.

What areas across Australia do you service?

We provide full architectural design, turf delivery, and master maintenance across Greater Metropolitan areas, coastal belts, and regional estates. Contact us directly to confirm rapid dispatch in your suburb.

What are your standard operating hours?

Our trade crews and dispatch office operate 7 days a week: Monday to Sunday from 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM. We offer flexible weekend consultation visits to suit working families.

How quickly can you commence a new turf or landscape project?

Site assessments can usually be arranged within 24 to 48 hours. Depending on materials sourcing and hardscaping scale, physical installation typically commences within 1 to 2 weeks from quotation acceptance.

Do you provide warranties on living plants and turf?

Yes. All turf installations come with a root-strike guarantee when maintained according to our complimentary customized watering schedule, and all hardscaping structures carry a statutory structural warranty.
